About

A conductor
who also listens

Born in 1983, raised in Limburg. My musical journey began as an oboist — first in Maastricht, then at the Royal Conservatoire in The Hague under Hans Roerade, principal oboist of the Residentie Orchestra. Alongside that, I studied wind band conducting in The Hague, graduating cum laude in 2011. Before that, I also studied Business Economics at Maastricht University.

In 2015, I won the International Conducting Competition Con Brio in Innsbruck. From 2016 to 2022, I served as chief conductor and general director of the Bundespolizeiorchester München, a large professional orchestra with more than ninety performances per season.

Since returning to the Netherlands, I conduct the Royal Netherlands Army Band Fanfare Bereden Wapens and serve as chief conductor of the Kerkraads Symphony Orchestra. I also conduct Philharmonie Sittard and serve as principal lecturer in wind band conducting at Conservatoire Maastricht (Zuyd).

In my work, I try to be leader, supporter and team player at once — with attention for both the process and the people in it.

Teaching & Coaching

Conservatoire
Maastricht

Principal Lecturer Wind Band Conducting — 2022–present

Since 2022, I have been principal lecturer in wind band conducting at Conservatoire Maastricht, following in the footsteps of my predecessors Jan Cober and Yvan Meylemans. In this role, I am responsible for the complete professional training of conductors for wind bands, fanfares and brass bands — from conducting technique and score study to rehearsal methodology and musical leadership. Since 2023, I also represent the conducting department and serve as a member of the curriculum committee.

Students follow a trajectory that goes beyond technique alone. How do you stand as a conductor in a rehearsal? What do you tell an orchestra — and what do you deliberately leave unsaid? How do you build an interpretation from the score? These questions are central to my teaching.

The Dutch music landscape is changing. The path from a child's first music lesson to conservatoire is no longer a matter of course in many places. For me, the solution lies in the chain: at primary school, where music education deserves a permanent place.

Conductor coaching

Alongside my work at the conservatoire, I offer individual coaching for conductors outside the regular training — from amateurs who want to grow to (semi-)professionals working on specific aspects of their conducting.

From baton technique to score analysis and rehearsal preparation. With attention to the connection between score and sound.

Rehearsal skills & leadership
