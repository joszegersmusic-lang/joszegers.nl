B

Biography

Jos Zegers (born 1983) started out as an oboist. Today he conducts a symphony orchestra, a military fanfare band and a wind orchestra, and trains new conductors at Conservatorium Maastricht.

He studied oboe in Maastricht and at the Royal Conservatoire in The Hague with Hans Roerade, principal oboe of the Residentie Orkest. In The Hague he also completed his studies in wind band conducting, graduating cum laude in 2011. Before that he studied Business Economics at Maastricht University.

In 2015 he won the international conducting competition Con Brio in Innsbruck. From 2016 to 2022 he was chief conductor and head of the Bundespolizeiorchester München, a professional orchestra giving more than ninety performances a season. As a guest conductor he has worked with the Polizeiorchester Bayern, the Münchener Symphoniker and the Bundespolizeiorchester Hannover, among others.

Back in the Netherlands he became conductor of the Fanfare Bereden Wapens and chief conductor of the Kerkraads Symfonie Orkest. He also conducts Philharmonie Sittard. He adjudicates at wind band competitions, for the KNMO in the Netherlands and the Bayerischer Blasmusikverband in Germany.

C

Teaching

Since 2022 Jos Zegers has been principal lecturer in wind band conducting at Conservatorium Maastricht (Zuyd University of Applied Sciences), a post previously held by Jan Cober and Yvan Meylemans.

He trains conductors for wind orchestra, fanfare band and brass band. Lessons focus on baton technique, score study and the rehearsal itself: how to prepare a work, what to tell an orchestra and what to leave out. Since 2023 he has represented the conducting department and sat on the curriculum committee.

He argues for structural music education in primary schools. That is where the path towards the conservatoire begins, and in many places that path can no longer be taken for granted.

Coaching for conductors

He also coaches conductors outside the conservatoire, one to one and tailored to their questions. That might be an amateur conductor who wants to develop further, or a professional working on one specific area: baton technique, score preparation or the structure of a rehearsal.

Projects

  • Maestro

    Podcast about conducting

    With conductor Sander Teepen he makes Maestro, a Dutch-language podcast about the craft: rehearsing, programming, competitions and the interplay between amateur and professional musicians. Guests have included Dominic Seldis, Johan de Meij and Ab Nieuwdorp.

    Listen to Maestro on Spotify

  • Pixie Dust

    Electronic music

    Under the name Pixie Dust he produces jazz-hop. His tracks have been streamed more than fifty million times. He has performed live at, among others, WMC 2022 in Kerkrade.
